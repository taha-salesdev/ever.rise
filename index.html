Rashan Program 🍚

Rozgar Program 💼

Why Ever Rise Exists

🙏

Dignity First

We believe every family deserves to eat with dignity. Our Rashan packages are delivered with respect, not just resources — ensuring recipients feel valued and supported.

🌱

Root Cause Focus

Food relief is a short-term fix. Through our Rozgar program, we aim to address the root cause of poverty by helping individuals earn a sustainable livelihood.

🔍

Case-by-Case Verification

Every case is personally verified by our team before any funds are allocated. We never distribute aid blindly — transparency and accountability are non-negotiable.

Our Mission

We believe true charity is not only helping people survive, but empowering them to become self-sufficient. Through our Rashan monthly food support initiative, Ever Rise directly addresses hunger in underprivileged communities across Bahawalpur and Punjab. Every rupee donated goes to a verified family in need — no middlemen, no waste, just direct impact.

How It Works

📋

Step 1

A family or individual submits their case via WhatsApp or a community reference.

🔎

Step 2

Our team visits and verifies the need — income status, family size, living conditions.

✅

Step 3

The case is approved and posted publicly. Donors can fund a specific case directly.

🚚

Step 4

Rashan is purchased and delivered personally to the family. Photos are shared for accountability.
