🌟 Featured: Our First Rashan Drive

In the early months of Ever Rise's journey, our founder Taha Bin Ishfaq personally identified a family of six living in a rented room near Airport Road, Bahawalpur. The father had recently lost his daily-wage job due to illness, leaving the family without income for over a month. With your generous support, we were able to deliver their first full Rashan package — a moment that reminded us all why Ever Rise exists. The mother's words were simple: "Aaj ghar mein roti bane gi" — "Today, bread will be made at home." That sentence is our purpose.

Case #1 Successfully Closed

August 2026 | Rashan Program

Our very first Rashan case — supporting a family of 6 — has been completed. A total of 5,000 PKR was raised through generous community donations and used to purchase and deliver a full month's grocery package. The family received flour, rice, lentils, oil, and sugar — enough to last the entire month. We are grateful to everyone who contributed.

Case #2 Completed — Orphan Household Supported

August 2026 | Rashan Program

Our second completed case involved a household caring for 3 orphaned children. The family had no regular income and was struggling with basic meals. We raised 7,000 PKR and delivered a comprehensive Rashan package, ensuring the children had nutritious meals. Their guardian shared that this was the first time in months the children had gone to sleep without hunger.
