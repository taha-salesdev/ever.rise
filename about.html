About Ever Rise NGO

Creating sustainable impact in Bahawalpur through compassion, empowerment, and opportunity.

Who We Are


Ever Rise is a humanitarian community NGO in Bahawalpur focused on helping deserving families build a better future. Our approach goes beyond temporary assistance by creating pathways toward self-reliance, hunger relief, and long-term economic stability.

🌍 Our Vision

A future where every family in Bahawalpur and beyond has access to basic necessities, financial independence, and opportunities to thrive.

🎯 Our Mission

To support vulnerable families through Rashan food assistance and empower individuals through Rozgar small business opportunities.

Our Core Values

🤝 Compassion

Serving humanity with empathy, respect, and kindness.

🌱 Empowerment

Helping people become self-sufficient and independent.

🛡 Integrity

Maintaining transparency, trust, and accountability.

"A Helping Hand Today, A Stronger Tomorrow."

Ever Rise is committed to creating lasting impact through sustainable support and community development.
