Message From Our Founder

Leading with compassion, dignity, and sustainable impact.

Founder • Ever Rise NGO

Building Hope Through Action

Ever Rise was established with a simple vision: to help families in Bahawalpur not only overcome immediate hardships but also build a path toward a better future.

Through our Rashan food drives and Rozgar small business programs, we strive to provide both relief and opportunity, ensuring that every contribution creates lasting impact.

Together, we can uplift communities, restore dignity, and empower individuals to become self-sufficient.

— Taha Bin Ishfaq - Founder & CEO, Ever Rise NGO
