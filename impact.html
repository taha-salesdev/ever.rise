Our Impact & Transparency

Every rupee you donate is tracked, verified, and put to direct use. We publish our activity summaries so you always know where your money goes.

Our Commitment to Accountability

Ever Rise operates on a zero-tolerance policy for misuse of donor funds. Our founder, Taha Bin Ishfaq, personally oversees each case — from verification to delivery. We believe that trust is the foundation of charity, and we work every day to earn and keep yours. Currently, all our active programs focus on Rashan (Monthly Food Support) for deserving families in Bahawalpur.

Rashan Program — Impact So Far

🍚

2 Cases Completed

Two Rashan cases have been successfully completed and closed. Each family received their monthly grocery package worth 5,000–7,000 PKR, covering essential food items including flour, rice, lentils, oil, and sugar.

📦

What's in a Rashan Package?

A standard Ever Rise Rashan package includes: 10kg Flour, 5kg Rice, 1kg Lentils (Daal), 2L Cooking Oil, 1kg Sugar, 1kg Salt, 500g Tea, and seasonal items as available.

✅

Verified Beneficiaries

All beneficiaries are verified in person. Our team checks income, family size, living conditions, and community references before any case is approved — no exceptions.

Where Your Money Goes

95%+

Direct to Beneficiaries

The overwhelming majority of every donation goes directly toward purchasing and delivering Rashan packages to verified families. No middlemen.

<5%

Operational Costs

A small fraction covers transport, packaging, and communication costs required to run the delivery process. This is kept to an absolute minimum.

0%

Administrative Salaries

Our founder and volunteers work on a fully voluntary basis. No salary or personal expense is drawn from donated funds — ever.
